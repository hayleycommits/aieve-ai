Questions cafés ask.

Can EVE take orders or bookings?
She takes the full request, such as a table, a cake order or a catering enquiry, and sends it to your team to confirm. If you would like her to take takeaway orders or connect to your booking system, we can build that for you, tailor-made.
Do we need a new phone number?
No. Your existing line forwards to EVE, at the times you choose.
Do callers know she’s an AI?
Yes. EVE says so, and she is honest when she doesn’t know something. She takes a message instead of guessing.
What about allergies?
EVE notes them on the request for your team. She never gives allergy advice herself.
How long does it take?
EVE is answering within 5 days of you saying yes.
Is there a contract?
No long contract. It is month to month, and you can leave with 30 days’ notice.
Can EVE be tailored to our café?
Yes. We can connect her to OpenTable and other booking systems, offer other languages, set up two agents so two calls are answered at once, take takeaway orders, and set her up for venues outside the UK. It is tailor-made, so we go through it with you when we speak.
