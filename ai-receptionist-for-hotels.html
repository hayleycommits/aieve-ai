How she fits in.

  1. Keep your number. Your line forwards to EVE. Guests ring the number they always have.
  2. Choose when she picks up. Every call, only when nobody else answers, or only when you’re closed.
  3. Your team gets the details. An email write-up after each call.
  4. Your team books. EVE takes the request. You confirm it in the booking system you already use, and take the payment. She never asks for card details.

Questions hotels ask.

Does EVE book rooms herself?
No. She takes the full request and sends it to your team, who confirm the room and take payment. That way nothing is double booked.
Do we need a new phone number?
No. Your existing line forwards to EVE, at the times you choose.
Do callers know she’s an AI?
Yes. EVE says so, and she is honest when she doesn’t know something. She takes a message instead of guessing.
How does she know about our hotel?
We set her up with your details: rooms, hours, check-in times, parking, dogs, the restaurant. If something changes, tell us or change it yourself on the dashboard plans.
How long does it take?
EVE is answering within 5 days of you saying yes.
Is there a contract?
No long contract. It is month to month, and you can leave with 30 days’ notice.
