Why hotels miss calls

The phone rings when you’re busiest with the guests you already have.

During breakfast and check-out

Everyone is serving, clearing or turning rooms. The caller gets voicemail and tries the next hotel.

In the evening

People plan trips after work. If reception closes at six, those calls ring out.

On closed days and out of season

Guests still ring to ask about dates, Christmas breaks and gift vouchers when nobody is in.

On the phone

What EVE handles for a hotel.

  • Room enquiries. Dates, nights, guests, dogs and special requests, taken down and sent to your team.
  • Restaurant tables. Requests from residents and non-residents alike.
  • Weddings, private dining and exclusive use. The date, the numbers and a call-back number, so no enquiry goes cold.
  • Everyday questions. Check-in times, parking, directions, breakfast and dogs, answered from the details you give us.
  • Messages. Late arrivals, suppliers and anything else, passed to the right person.
How it fits in

Nothing to replace.

  1. Keep your number. Your line forwards to EVE. Guests ring the number they always have.
  2. You choose when she picks up. Every call, only when nobody else answers, or only when you’re closed.
  3. Your team gets the details. An email write-up after each call, and on the dashboard plans, a list of who is waiting to hear back.
  4. Your team books. EVE takes the request. You confirm it in the booking system you already use.

Questions hotels ask

Good to know.

Does EVE book rooms herself?

No. She takes the full request and sends it to your team, who confirm the room and take payment. That way nothing is double booked.

Do we need a new phone number?

No. Your existing line forwards to EVE, at the times you choose.

Do callers know she’s an AI?

Yes. EVE says so, and she is honest when she doesn’t know something. She takes a message instead of guessing.

How does she know about our hotel?

We set her up with your details: rooms, hours, check-in times, parking, dogs, the restaurant. If something changes, tell us or change it yourself on the dashboard plans.

How long does it take?

EVE is answering within 5 days of you saying yes.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. It is month to month, and you can leave with 30 days’ notice.
