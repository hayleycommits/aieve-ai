Privacy

How we look after your information.

What we collect when you book a demo, talk to EVE or email us, and the rights you have.

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information AI EVE collects through aieve.ai, why, how long we keep it and the rights you have. We keep it as short and plain as we can.

Who we are

The controller of your personal information is Hayley Moses, trading as AI EVE, based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us about anything in this policy at hello@aieve.ai.

What we collect

When you book a demo

Our booking calendar is run by LeadConnector (GoHighLevel). When you book, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name and the time you chose, plus anything you add in the notes.

When you talk to EVE on this website

The “Talk to EVE” voice demo uses your microphone. The call is recorded and transcribed. It runs on Vapi, with the audio carried by Daily.co, your speech turned into text by Deepgram, EVE’s replies written by OpenAI and her voice produced by Cartesia. We receive the recording, the transcript and basic call details such as the date, time and length. Please don’t share sensitive information during the demo.

When you email us

If you write to hello@aieve.ai, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach.

When you visit the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps standard server logs, which include your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and protect the service. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Running the demo and answering your enquiry. We use your booking details, demo recording and transcript, and emails to run the demo, reply to you and follow up if you ask us to. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us and showing how EVE works.
  • Improving the demo. We may review demo transcripts to check EVE answered properly and to improve her. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • Keeping the site secure. Hosting logs help keep the site running and protected from misuse. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • Where we ask for consent, for example if you tick a box to hear from us, we rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by emailing us.

You can object to any use based on legitimate interests (see your rights below).

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the service providers that run the features above: LeadConnector (GoHighLevel) for bookings; Vapi, Daily.co, Deepgram, OpenAI and Cartesia for the voice demo; our email provider for emails; and GitHub for hosting. They process it on our behalf. We do not sell your information, and we don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

International transfers

Several of these providers are based in the United States, so your information may be processed outside the UK. Where that happens we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Demo call recordings and transcripts: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Booking details and emails: for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, then up to 2 years.
  • Hosting logs: kept by GitHub under its own retention rules.

If you become a client, we keep what we need for the contract and our accounts for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it if it’s wrong;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to us using it, including where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask for it in a portable format, where that applies;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email hello@aieve.ai. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113.

Automated decisions

EVE is an AI, and her replies in the demo are generated automatically. We don’t make any decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies. The booking calendar on our Book a demo page is provided by LeadConnector. It doesn’t load, and sets no cookies, until you choose “Show the booking calendar”; once loaded it sets the cookies it needs to show times and take your booking. The voice demo may store small technical data in your browser while the call runs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
